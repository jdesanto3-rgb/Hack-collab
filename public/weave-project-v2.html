PROJECT BRIEF
OVERVIEW

Neura Nova Landing Page

Full website redesign for Neura Nova Inc., a B2B AI platform targeting enterprise operators and technical decision-makers. Primary goal: reduce time-to-trust for dual personas. Dual-CTA structure with research-backed social proof placement above fold.

CLIENT & TEAM

Stakeholders

Client: Neura Nova Inc. — Sarah Lin (VP Product), James Park (CTO)
ECD: J. DeSanto  ·  Design: A. Kumar  ·  Strategy: M. Ramos
Engagement type: Brand + Digital Design · 6-week sprint

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Brand intent

Authoritative yet approachable. Dark hero with dual CTA — confidence for operators, trust signals for buyers. Playfair Display for editorial gravitas, Inter for legibility at scale. Brand orange (#D96000) as the single action colour.
